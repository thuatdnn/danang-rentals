Frequently asked questions

Do I pay any agent fee?

No. Our service is free for tenants – the landlord pays us.

What is the deposit and minimum lease?

Usually 1 month deposit, paid directly to the owner. Minimum lease is 3 months unless stated.

Why is there no exact address?

We share the exact address when your viewing is booked, to protect owners' privacy.

Can you help with temporary residence registration?

Yes. We guide you and the landlord through registration and give you an English contract.

Can't find the right place?

Send us your budget and dates – we'll reply with matching apartments, including ones not online yet.
